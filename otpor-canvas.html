Comparative research / research track 05

From Gene Sharp to Otpor. From Otpor to CANVAS. Where does CJP fit?

The Serbian movement's debt to nonviolent-action scholarship is historically documented. The same level of evidence has not been found for a CANVAS–CJP relationship.

Our finding: similarity in techniques deserves investigation, but it is not proof of ideological transmission, financing or command.

Independent investigation · 18 October 2026 · Source-limited review
01 / What is verified

A documented transmission. An unproved connection.

Sharp → Otpor → CANVAS

Historically documented

Otpor veterans Srđa Popović and Slobodan Đinović wrote that Robert Helvey brought Gene Sharp's books to them in April 2000. Their account also makes clear that Otpor had organised before this meeting. The former Otpor activists later founded CANVAS.

Original testimony: Popović and Đinović on Sharp

CANVAS → CJP

Unresolved, no direct evidence identified

Reviewed public records have not supplied a CANVAS training roster naming CJP organisers, authenticated correspondence, financial transfer records, a workshop record or a direct acknowledgement by the relevant individuals. Shared tactics are not a substitute for these records.

No evidence identified is not proof that contact never occurred.

02 / Historical sequence

Read the dates before drawing arrows.

1973

Gene Sharp, The Politics of Nonviolent Action

Three-volume work on power, the 198-method taxonomy and the dynamics of campaigns. AEI reference list.

1993

From Dictatorship to Democracy

Strategic manual first developed in the context of Burma's democratic movement. Its general framework is not proof that a particular movement read it. AEI library.

1998–2000

Otpor, Serbia

Serbian resistance movement made use of irony, youthful imagery and nonviolent mobilisation against the Milošević government.

Late April 2000

Documented exposure to Sharp's works

Popović and Đinović retrospectively describe receiving Sharp books through Robert Helvey. Their own chronology rules out portraying every earlier Otpor tactic as originating from that encounter. First-person account.

2007

CANVAS Core Curriculum

Published guide covers strategic planning, pillars of support, communication, movement management and nonviolent discipline. Original curriculum (PDF).

2015

Blueprint for Revolution

Book by Popović with Matthew Miller interprets tactics across international examples. This review cites the publisher's description, not an authenticated full-text book analysis.

16 May 2026 onward

CJP develops in India

Online satire evolves into education protests and later electoral-roll demonstrations. Direct authorship, formal training or funding from CANVAS is not documented. CJP chronology.

04 / Public words in context

“Regime change”: three distinct propositions.

15 October: Prashant Bhushan

ThePrint attributed a statement about citizens' right to seek “regime change” to Bhushan at the joint press conference. It is his reported public position, not proof that the CJP organisation instructed or adopted it.

Reported conference remarks

16 October: Abhijeet Dipke

Dipke reportedly clarified that CJP's stated demand was removal of the Chief Election Commissioner, not a campaign for replacing the government. This is an attributed organisational claim, not an independently audited guarantee of private intentions.

Dipke's reported clarification

Government and opposition accusations

Statements claiming a destabilisation agenda are political allegations until supported by original operational evidence. Lawful advocacy for a change of government through elections must not be confused with a plot involving violence, unlawful financing or clandestine foreign control.

07 / What would change the finding?

Required evidence for a CANVAS–CJP connection.

  • Authentic participant records identifying a CANVAS or AEI workshop and a CJP organiser, with date and place.
  • Original communication in which an organiser documents training, strategic advice or adoption of a specific published curriculum.
  • Verifiable financial records linking an identified institution and CJP recipient, not a generic funding allegation.
  • Corroborated firsthand testimony, cross-checked with original documents, showing what assistance occurred.
  • Any substantial counterevidence or denial from identifiable organisations, recorded alongside the claim.

Current classification: CANVAS-to-CJP operational direction, funding, institutional training and ideological influence are not established by the reviewed public record. The investigation remains open to direct contradictory evidence.
