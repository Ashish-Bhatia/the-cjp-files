CANVAS → CJP

A shared repertoire of symbols, satire, coalition building and assemblies is not a documented training, finance or command relationship. No authenticated direct institutional transfer appears in the public material reviewed.

This conclusion is limited to available evidence. It does not claim any contact is impossible.

1973
The Politics of Nonviolent Action

Gene Sharp's three-volume work describes power, the famous 198-method taxonomy and the dynamics of struggle. The method list is descriptive, not an attribution engine.

1993
From Dictatorship to Democracy

Sharp's strategic guide emerged from work concerning Burma's democratic struggle. It discusses planning, pillars of support and self-liberation. Similar methods used in India do not alone establish an Indian dictatorship.

An important political dispute

Whose “regime change”?

Prashant Bhushan / 15 October

A public political argument

Bhushan publicly defended citizens' right to seek a change of government. His view does not establish that CJP as an organisation adopted it or a particular means of change.

Press conference reporting ↗
Abhijeet Dipke / 16 October

A narrower stated demand

Dipke reportedly distinguished the demand for the Chief Election Commissioner's removal from replacing the elected government. This is his publicly stated position, not proof of every person's private intention.

What would establish outside coordination?

Authenticated training records, dated acknowledgments, original operational communications or a verified financial link. Neither a familiar tactic nor a foreign endorsement reaches that threshold.
