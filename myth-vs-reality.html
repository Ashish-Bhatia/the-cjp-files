What holds up?

The collection focuses on remarks attributable to speeches, press conferences, press bytes and interviews. A speaker's statement is not automatically a verified fact. Paraphrases are identified through their published source.

Important distinction

An assessment may verify that a person made a claim while leaving the underlying allegation unproven. Police allegations, political denials and procedural rulings require separate scrutiny.
