The CJP Files / Investigation 002 / 15 October 2026

Myth.
Reality.
Evidence.

Twenty-six propositions under scrutiny. Some originate with the movement. Others are accusations made about it. Every one is tested against what the published record establishes, what contradicts it, and what remains to be proved.

Editorial standard
No shortcuts.

A political slogan is not a fact. A newspaper report is not a court ruling. A past association is not a current command chain. An absent document is not proof of innocence or guilt.

Evidence cutoff / 15 OCT 2026, IST
01 / Court record

The September settlement has exceptions.

The Supreme Court closed specified protest FIRs while preserving a narrow exception and requiring additional government action. The complete terms matter.

02 / Associations

History is not present-day control.

Former AAP activities among CJP organisers are reported. A current AAP or AEI instruction chain has not been established.

03 / Electoral rolls

A headline total is not a wrongful-vote total.

Draft non-inclusion, final deletion and loss of eligibility differ. The election-roll controversy requires state-level reconciliation.

Origins & identity

Reporting dates the remarks to 15 May and Dipke’s online launch to 16 May. The precise meaning and context of the remarks are contested; the subsequent clarification matters.

What would settle it

09 / 26Misleading inference; underlying totals need audit
Elections

Around 130 million names were removed, so 130 million eligible voters were disenfranchised.

Media reports a very large cumulative roll change. A draft omission, final deletion, duplicate correction and proven loss of a qualified person’s vote are different categories. The numerical total cannot be equated directly to wrongful disenfranchisement.

What the record supports

Government states SIR includes draft publication, objections and appeals. Reuters reports concerns and opposing claims. Neither substitutes for state-level reconciled rolls and audited wrongful exclusions.

What would settle it

State-by-state before/draft/final bridge, deduplication, restored names, eligible-person sample

Publication discipline

How these verdicts are reached.

Proposals are not facts.

Normative demands are analysed for legal feasibility and effects, not marked “false” merely because an editor disagrees with them.

Allegations need originals.

Source links permit readers to inspect context. Where we lack the original order, dataset, audited financial record or authenticated transcript, the verdict remains provisional.

People deserve precision.

Each link distinguishes office, endorsement, attendance, former affiliation, contested allegation and direct finance. Read our editorial standards.
