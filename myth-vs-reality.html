The CJP Files / Investigation 002 / 15 October 2026

Myth.
Reality.
Evidence.

45 publicly attributable statements under scrutiny. Some originate with the movement. Others are accusations made about it. Every one is tested against what the published record establishes, what contradicts it, and what remains to be proved.

Editorial standard
No shortcuts.

A political slogan is not a fact. A newspaper report is not a court ruling. A past association is not a current command chain. An absent document is not proof of innocence or guilt.

Evidence cutoff / 15 OCT 2026, IST
01 / Court record

The September settlement has exceptions.

The Supreme Court closed specified protest FIRs while preserving a narrow exception and requiring additional government action. The complete terms matter.

02 / Associations

History is not present-day control.

Former AAP activities among CJP organisers are reported. A current AAP or AEI instruction chain has not been established.

03 / Electoral rolls

A headline total is not a wrongful-vote total.

Draft non-inclusion, final deletion and loss of eligibility differ. The election-roll controversy requires state-level reconciliation.

The evidence test

Questions worth asking.
Claims worth testing.

Inclusion rule: only statements made in a speech, press conference, press byte / press interaction or interview. Public posts, website manifestos and unattributed reporting are not treated as CJP statements. Media reports of remarks are clearly attributed; full recordings remain an authentication gap. Evidence-testing records may include legislation, orders and datasets. This is a bounded sample, not a transcript of every appearance. A reported allegation is never a finding of wrongdoing. Reviews based on edited interviews or media descriptions visibly state their provenance.
