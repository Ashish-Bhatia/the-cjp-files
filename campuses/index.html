05 / Students & universities

A student coalition, not a university conspiracy

Student organisations are named in multiple protests. Institutions themselves require distinct evidence of endorsement.

Who is documented

AISA figures took part in October protests and appeared with CJP leadership at a public Delhi event. The July Parliament march also included student activists and hunger strikers, alongside wider civilian and opposition participants. Reporting records the visible actions without establishing that university administrations sanctioned them. S17↗ S34↗

On 9 October at Itarsi, at least 14 students travelling from Pune were reportedly detained; reporting identified AISF, NSYF and RRR participants. In Bengaluru on 17 October, AISA members and social activists protested near Town Hall before police detained participants. These episodes support the existence of multiple student networks in the wider mobilisation. S28↗ S29↗

A documented institutional response did occur in Maharashtra: Savitribai Phule Pune University declined permission for CJP to hold a 17 October demonstration on campus, citing a Management Council policy restricting non-university protest events. Organisers relocated the demonstration to the Collector's Office. The reporting establishes an institutional refusal, not endorsement of the protest or evidence of conspiracy. The underlying university resolution remains to be collected. S45↗

What has not been shown

No verified document in this edition establishes institutional authorisation, university funding or executive direction by JNU, Delhi University, Jamia, Aligarh Muslim University, Hyderabad University, Jadavpur University or any IIT for CJP's October campaign.

The presence of a student, former student or union leader does not transfer responsibility to an entire campus. Nor does an organisational connection between a student federation and a political party prove that every participant is following a party instruction. Event notices, institutional circulars and university responses must be read separately.

The campus evidence threshold

Future specific campus entries require a dated organiser notice, independent account of the occurrence, precise university premises, any permission or disciplinary order, alleged damage records, and participant or administration responses. This first edition does not publish a national campus map inflated with locations lacking those records.
