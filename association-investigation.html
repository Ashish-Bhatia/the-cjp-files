Investigation 003 / People, parties and student networks

The coalition question.

Yogendra Yadav. Prashant Bhushan. The Left student organisations. AAP. Where did they support CJP? Where did they organise together? And where do claims of command or covert sponsorship go beyond the evidence?

Central finding
Open coordination is documented. Centralised control is not.

The strongest evidence is the 7 October joint student press conference. Former AAP relationships, an invitation reported through intermediaries, and solidarity marches are separate kinds of links.

Examine the record ↗
The distinction that matters

Four relationships. Four standards of proof.

A public endorsement is neither a budget transaction nor an organisational appointment. A joint press conference is evidence of an openly shared objective, not necessarily of a common command. The investigation codes each relationship according to the conduct reported and the strength of its primary or secondary source.

Documented

Joint mobilisation

CJP, AISA, AISF and SFI made a joint protest call on 7 October. ASAP, identified as AAP's student wing, was also reported present.

Documented history

Former AAP links

Dipke, Ranka and other named CJP figures have different types of earlier AAP association. Previous service, volunteering and supporting speech are not interchangeable.

Not established

A central instruction chain

Available public material does not demonstrate a current AAP, Congress, AEI or Left-party executive controlling CJP's operations, finances or decisions.

Historical association / 2015–2016

Former AAP leaders. Not current AAP delegates.

The relationship between Yogendra Yadav, Prashant Bhushan and AAP is not merely ideological. It has a documented organisational history followed by a documented rupture. Neither earlier office nor later dissent determines present relationships without fresh evidence.

March–April 2015

The rupture with AAP

Yogendra Yadav and Prashant Bhushan, among AAP's founding leadership, lost their Political Affairs Committee responsibilities on 4 March, were removed from the National Executive on 28 March, and were expelled from party membership on 20 April. The conflict involved disputed internal processes and competing public explanations.

2 October 2016

A separate political organisation

The two helped develop Swaraj Abhiyan after their AAP expulsion. In October 2016, a distinct electoral party, Swaraj India, was launched. Historical membership and a political split in 2015 must not be misread as AAP instructions to either activist during the 2026 CJP mobilisation.

Investigative judgment

What would change the assessment?

Stronger allegations demand stronger original evidence. These are the tests for any claim that a public ally was also an operational sponsor, agent or controller.

01

Authority

Signed appointments, formal delegation, minutes documenting who approved CJP actions and budgets.

02

Money

Accounts, bank records, disbursements, invoices and authorisations. No AEI, foreign-funding or party-financing allegation is adopted without evidence.

03

Commands

Authenticated dated instructions between organisations, not similarity of rhetoric, common ideology, party history or media appearances.

04

Independent checks

Complete recordings, conflicting testimony, police or court material, independent attendance counts and the right to respond.

Gene Sharp / 198-method crosswalk

A familiar method does not establish an imported playbook.

The 7 October joint CJP press conference is another occurrence of #1, Public Speeches, a method already documented before this investigation. Student and allied assemblies must be attributed to their actual organisers, not automatically re-coded as CJP-directed.
