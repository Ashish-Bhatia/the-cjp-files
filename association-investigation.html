Who appeared together, who once worked together, and who endorsed the campaign? A record of dated public ties, with the difference between collaboration and control kept intact.

A matter of definitions

Being in the same
room is not command.

Direct involvement

Participation

An individual attended, spoke at, or helped convene an identified gathering. This is evidence of a public act.

Documented association

Prior affiliation

A person previously held an office, job or volunteer role. Historical contact does not establish a current instruction chain.

Higher proof threshold

Direction or finance

Claims of present control or transfers need original governance, instruction or financial records, not proximity.

What public mobilisation establishes
