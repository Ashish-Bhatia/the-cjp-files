01 / A narrower question

A meeting is evidence.
An allegation is not.

The CJP movement formed in 2026. The 2014–25 careers of its members and of political figures are context, not CJP events. To add a named direct-contact record, an independent source must document a meeting or co-appearance with identifiable participants. Joint organisational announcements are recorded separately.

05 / Editorial evidence threshold

Keep contact,
control and finance separate.

A photo can prove co-presence. Minutes, authenticated messages or corroborated testimony can establish coordination. An actual financial transfer requires a recipient, source, date and independently verifiable record. An individual's party association or a group's public demonstration is not sufficient.

Independently sourced

PTI, AFP, SIPA/Reuters Connect, Indian Express photojournalists, and contemporaneous venue-specific reporting anchor the documented entries. Identifications follow published photo captions; the investigation has not independently authenticated original camera files.

Where research remains incomplete

Original meeting minutes, membership records, signed joint organising documents, unpublished attendee lists, any completed Congress–CJP conversation, and claimed foreign institutional contacts remain unverified in the current evidence set.
