How the organisation changed

On 3 June, Saurav Das, Ashutosh Ranka and Vijeta Dahiya were presented in public communication roles. Dahiya was removed on 21 July; he later challenged the decision in an interview.

On 6 August, the group announced a national committee headed by Dipke, with Das and Ranka as co-convenors. The public roster identifies functional and regional roles. A named post is evidence of a public declaration, not independent proof of constitutional incorporation, employment contracts or internal governance.
