01 / The origin question

From online satire
to the streets.

What the contemporary record establishes, and where it ends.

The earliest independently reported launch date is 16 May 2026. Contemporary accounts credit communications professional Abhijeet Dipke with starting the Cockroach Janta Party after public controversy over courtroom remarks attributed to Chief Justice Surya Kant. The remarks were made on 15 May. The Chief Justice issued a clarification the following day, saying he had criticised people entering professions through false qualifications, not Indian youth generally.

The first website used satire to discuss employment and representation, while also listing demands on electoral protection, judicial accountability, women's representation, political defection and media independence. The original May 16 post and complete first website archive have not yet been authenticated in this review.

On 3 June, CJP publicly announced spokespersons Saurav Das, Vijeta Dahiya and Ashutosh Ranka. On 6 June, Reuters reported a street protest led by Dipke over examination administration. On 6 August, CJP announced a National Working Committee and reiterated its intention to act as a pressure group rather than an electoral party.

04 / The standard of proof

What remains
unresolved.

Documentation gaps are part of the result, not footnotes to conceal.

Original digital origins

The dated first May post and complete archive of the original website remain outstanding. Copycat websites must not be treated as authenticated CJP publications.

Electoral registration and money

A dated ECI party-register search and public financing records are required before publishing categorical claims about registration, income or third-party funding.

Police and court proceedings

Delhi custody records, FIR identifiers, the Goa habeas decision and other signed orders have not been obtained in this phase.

Geographic completeness

The current register contains selected independently reported events. No all-state or all-language systematic coverage has been completed.

Electoral-roll figures

The widely reported 2026 SIR deletion numbers require an official state-wise denominator and distinction between draft, final, corrected and restored entries.

International comparison

Gene Sharp's published techniques describe common public protest forms. Similarity of technique is not evidence of foreign financing, training or operational direction.
