03 / The Gene Sharp question

Same technique.
Different origins?

Sharp's framework describes speech, placards, vigils, marches, assemblies and many other nonviolent methods. Such activity has a long independent history in India.

The reviewed record supports 16 numbered matches. Evidence of direct AEI or CANVAS financing, training or operational direction of CJP remains unestablished.

The distinction

Tactical resemblance is evidence of resemblance, not a relationship.

05 / Competing explanations

What is known.
What remains contested.

Documented in open records
  • 01
    Organisers and public protests

    CJP's founders, speaking events and reported demonstrations are traceable to dated accounts.

  • 02
    Visible cross-group participation

    Student bodies, lawyers and politicians have appeared at events, sometimes also holding separate demonstrations.

  • 03
    A historical Sharp–Otpor–CANVAS connection

    Otpor leaders have described exposure to Sharp's writings, and former Otpor activists created CANVAS.

Claims without demonstrated proof
  • 01
    A single command structure

    Shared opposition to an issue does not itself establish who directs whom.

  • 02
    AEI or CANVAS direction of CJP

    No original verified training, financing or operational communication has been produced in the material reviewed.

  • 03
    A final ruling on disputed allegations

    Police assertions, campaign allegations, legal pleadings and political speech have different evidentiary weights.

How to read this investigation

News reporting establishes what was observed or attributed. It does not verify every speaker's allegation. A court filing is a party's account unless a judicial order adopts it. An untested hypothesis is labelled as such.
