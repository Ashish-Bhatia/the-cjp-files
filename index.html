07 / How we work

The standards behind
the investigation.

01 — Trace the claim

No guilt by adjacency.

A shared protest, public statement, common grievance, or political endorsement is recorded as such. It is not silently upgraded into membership, financing, covert collaboration, or illegality.

02 — Compare explanations

Test the competing story.

We distinguish direct coordination, intellectual influence, independent tactical adoption and insufficient evidence. Official claims and protester claims receive the same scrutiny.

03 — Keep the record correctable

Publish what is known.

Sources, dates, uncertainty and missing records remain visible. Allegations of criminal conduct or foreign sponsorship require original documents, corroboration, and a fair right of response.
