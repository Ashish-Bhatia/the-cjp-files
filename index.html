THE EDITOR'S NOTE / 01

The evidence should decide the story.

The Cockroach Janta Party emerged from political satire and became a visible participant in 2026 student and electoral protests. Its allies, finances, methods and opponents deserve scrutiny. Some relationships are openly documented. Other accusations, including foreign direction and undeclared funding, remain unproven in the records examined here.

We distinguish peaceful political demands from criminal conduct, an FIR from a conviction, and a familiar protest tactic from proof of hidden coordination. The initial edition follows original institutional records where available and contemporaneous independent reporting where necessary.
