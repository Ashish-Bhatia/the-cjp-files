About / Scope and standards

The method
matters.

An editorial inquiry into the public evidence surrounding the Cockroach Janta Party, its opponents and supporters, and comparisons to the strategic nonviolent action literature.

Purpose

Questions, not
presumed guilt.

The CJP Files examines documented public conduct and speech from multiple political perspectives. It checks movement claims, state responses, courts and electoral disputes using the same evidentiary standard.

The site is unaffiliated with CJP, political parties, AEI or CANVAS. Its title frames a subject of inquiry, not an allegation that a foreign institution directs an Indian movement.

The threshold

What the record establishes

First, what happened or what was said. Second, whether the underlying statement is supported or contradicted. Third, what cannot be concluded from the available material.

What the record does not establish

A criminal conspiracy, undisclosed foreign financing, a secret command chain or illegality merely because people appeared together or used familiar protest techniques.

Our editorial tests

Five rules for every claim.

  1. 01
    Identify an original, dated source

    A court order, official record, full public speech or attributed reporting. Record its publication context and time.

  2. 02
    Separate speech from events

    An allegation is attributed to its speaker. A protest announcement is not logged as a completed gathering.

  3. 03
    Check opposing evidence

    Police accounts, responses by organisers, court directions, alternative witnesses and observable context must be assessed together.

  4. 04
    Describe the limits

    Edited interviews, secondhand reports, conflicting attendance estimates and pending litigation have visible qualifications.

  5. 05
    Correct the record

    New original evidence should change conclusions. Prior publication does not make a claim final.

Corrections and right of reply

Challenge a
finding.

If a dated fact, quotation, affiliation or source is wrong, identify the page and passage, explain the correction and attach a verifiable primary source. Allegations about an identifiable person receive particular scrutiny.

To submit a public correction using GitHub, follow the repository's issue route. Do not publish phone numbers, private addresses, confidential records or sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Submit a correction on GitHub ↗

The issue form is hosted by GitHub and may require an account or repository issue access.

A suitable correction includes
  • The exact page and disputed wording.
  • The event or publication date.
  • A public URL to original evidence.
  • Specific contradictory or contextual facts.
  • The correction requested and why it matters.

Editorial changes are evaluated against source evidence. Publication of a submission does not imply acceptance of an allegation.

Rights and public responsibility

Fairness is a
publication standard.

The site links to original third-party sources rather than reproducing copyrighted articles. An absence of evidence is not proof of innocence or misconduct. Electoral fraud, abusive policing, illegal finance and foreign-direction allegations require individual, documented examination rather than rhetorical association.

Standing conclusion

Visible protest collaboration and tactical resemblance are documented. A covert AEI–CANVAS–CJP operation is not established in the reviewed public evidence.
