What this publication
stands for.

A public-interest account of CJP's origins, conduct, organisational relationships, legal disputes and political context, independent of the movement and its critics.

PUBLIC-SOURCE JOURNALISM · 19 OCTOBER 2026

Our purpose

The CJP Files documents public events, reported roles, political contacts, institutional decisions and disputed claims concerning the Cockroach Janta Party, also written Cockroach Janata Party. It does not speak on behalf of CJP, government authorities, any political party, or any international civil-resistance organisation.

Its starting point is straightforward: a verifiable statement is different from a verifiable event; a public connection is different from a private decision-making chain. The same standard applies to the movement's own claims and those made against it.

The editorial rules

Distinguish events, allegations and judgments

Police allegations, statements by politicians, protester accounts, signed judicial decisions, official notices and independent witness reports are described according to their origin. A complaint or detention does not equal a conviction.

Separate types of association

A named office, past party affiliation, public endorsement, shared protest and direct funding transfer are materially different. Each requires its own dated evidence. Individuals attending demonstrations in an ordinary capacity are not made subjects of personal profiles.

Preserve context and opposing evidence

Original context, the date a source was published, the date of the event, later responses and source reliability all affect the conclusion. Anonymous claims and social-media screenshots are leads, not final findings.

Apply the same standards to every side

Institutional accounts are not presumed correct because they are official; movement allegations are not presumed correct because they concern civil liberties. Relevant statutes, court rulings and credible eyewitness evidence are assessed together.

Protect ordinary participants and private information

Public roles are named where necessary for accountability and context. This publication does not assemble identity records of ordinary attendees, personal addresses, unpublished communications or confidential banking information.

Corrections

Corrections should identify the precise passage, a dated primary source or credible conflicting publication, and the proposed factual change. Substantive changes should be publicly noted with their basis rather than silently overwritten.

To request a correction or provide public documentary material, use the repository's issue tracker. Do not post private identifying information or unsupported allegations about individuals.

What is not claimed

No review of public records can prove every unseen financial relationship, communication or motive. The reporting covers identified events and materials through 19 October 2026, not a complete census of all local demonstrations. Incomplete court records and conflicting attendance estimates are identified where consequential.

Parallel use of well-known protest methods does not prove that CJP was trained, financed or directed by Gene Sharp's Albert Einstein Institution, CANVAS or a foreign government. Such a conclusion would require direct, authentic evidence.

Publication

Independent by design

The CJP Files is not the Cockroach Janta Party's official website and does not claim access to its internal communications.
