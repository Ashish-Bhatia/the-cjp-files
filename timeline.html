18 October 2026

Das and Ranka release proceedings remain contested

CJP representatives complained of rejected sureties and delays. At publication the dispute was reported rather than resolved by a final verified order. Their lawyers' allegations are not findings against the authorities.

18 October 2026

Dipke says CJP will determine its next steps after colleagues' release

In public remarks carried by news agency ANI, Dipke described plans to regroup once Das and Ranka returned. This was an intention, not evidence of a new meeting or demonstration already taking place.

Hindustan Times / ANI ↗
17 October 2026, 02:42 IST (report published)

Custody access unresolved overnight: AISA traces Bora, CJP seeks Dipke's location

Mint's early-morning report says AISA traced Bora to RK Puram police station, while CJP reported lacking verified information about Dipke and complained of obstacles to legal contact. Conditions of custody, transfer history and eventual release were not independently established at publication; do not label individuals disappeared or released as fact.

Mint / 17 October early-morning source-attributed custody reports ↗
17 October 2026

Dipke released subject to a peace condition

A Delhi Special Executive Magistrate ordered CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke's release on conditions following detention before the Connaught Place protest. This order is distinct from proceedings involving Das and Ranka.

LiveLaw, court report ↗
17 October 2026

Pune holds a separate ‘Lehra Do Tiranga’ demonstration

At the Ambedkar statue near Pune's collectorate, CJP-convened protesters displayed flags and placards, spoke about the right to protest and concluded with the national anthem. Reporters described a peaceful gathering without detentions. A separate nearby Jawab Do gathering must not be treated as a CJP event.

Indian Express, on-site reporting ↗
16 October 2026

AAP holds distinct Tiranga march near central Delhi party offices

The New Indian Express/PTI reports AAP activists separately held a national-flag march by its central Delhi headquarters and near Rashtrapati Bhavan. Public political solidarity is established; financial, command or formal organisational integration with CJP is not.

New Indian Express / PTI regional report ↗
16 October 2026

AISA president Neha Bora separately detained while heading to march

Reports place Bora's detention at Mandi House, distinct from Dipke's detention. AISA later raised custody and counsel-access concerns. Her group affiliation and independent detention must be retained separately from CJP central operations.

Indian Express / reported police and AISA accounts ↗
16 October 2026, approximately 17:00–19:00 IST

Connaught Place participants visibly gather with tricolours despite Central Park closure

Indian Express eyewitness reporting photographs flag-carriers at Palika Bazaar and near the sealed Central Park. Early reporter estimate approximately 1,000, later report approximately 2,000; neither represents an audited count. Confirms the 15 October Sharp #18 conditional-repeat prediction. Demonstrated street gathering differs from opening the banned park or completing original programme.

Indian Express / two independent on-location reports and photographs ↗
16 October 2026, approximately 16:50 IST

Delhi Police preventively detains CJP founder before Connaught Place march

Dipke was photographed and reported being detained after leaving his Karol Bagh lodging to join the planned event. This prevents his personal participation at the planned site, not proof that the entire proposed mobilisation never occurred. Bail/custody basis and any judicial order remain subject to verification.

Indian Express / on-location photo and reporter account ↗
16 October 2026

Delhi Police serves Dipke notice against planned 17:00 Central Park protest

Police served written prohibitory-order and permit notice at Dipke's temporary residence, citing Section 163 BNSS, crowd density, traders and access concerns. Live filmed conversation records Dipke disputing the premise that tricolour display is unlawful. Separate notice/legal restrictions from claims that the national flag itself was banned.

Indian Express / reported police notice and observed livestream ↗
