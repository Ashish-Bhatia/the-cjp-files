Who is organising the protests?

Public leadership is identifiable. Internal governance, accounts and membership boundaries are less visible.

A movement using the language of a party

Cockroach Janta Party describes a nationwide political mobilisation but its public label should not be confused with proof of registration as an electoral party. Reuters identifies Dipke as founder. Indian Express separately names Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka in organising and spokesperson roles. These are public roles, not evidence of a formal chain of command beyond what is disclosed. S35↗ S30↗ S07↗

On 6 June, organisers negotiated a police-permitted gathering at Jantar Mantar. By 25 July, representatives were engaged in public government negotiations. On 23 August the group announced a National Working Committee meeting for the following day, signalling an attempt to develop internal organisation after the summer agitation. The meeting notice is documented; certified minutes or membership resolutions were not obtained. S02↗ S07↗ S09↗

What changed in October

The October campaign called for the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar and challenged the Election Commission's Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls. Das discussed the planned 10 October mobilisations and apprehensions of detention. CJP and AISA representatives later publicly called for a 16 October tricolour demonstration. These are concrete examples of open organising and announced protest coordination. S10↗ S30↗ S17↗

A 15 October news conference publicly presented movement leaders beside representatives of AISA, SFI and other civil society voices. A press conference shows cooperation on messaging; it does not automatically establish common legal ownership, fundraising arrangements, instruction rights or secret direction. S17↗

The missing organisation file

To determine what the label 'party' covers, investigators need original registrations, written constitutions if adopted, governing body records, delegated financial authorities, actual bank and payment disclosures, and documents defining the relationship between local chapters and the national convenor.

The current evidence establishes public organisers and successive campaigns. It does not yet support a verified chart of every city-level chapter or a reliable tally of members. No such estimates appear on this site.

Source record & limitations

Source labels open the underlying publisher's document. Published reports and attributed statements are distinguished from original official records. No source is described as independently authenticated when it has not been obtained.
