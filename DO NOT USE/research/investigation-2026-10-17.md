## 16 October Delhi sequence and disputed counts

1. **Before the 17:00 proposed gathering** Delhi Police served a notice at the Karol Bagh residence where Abhijeet Dipke was staying, citing Section 163 of the BNSS, lack of venue permission, congested shopping precinct and emergency/trader access. Dipke publicly challenged the notion that display of the Tricolour was prohibited in a livestreamed police encounter. The encounter is **publicly observed background**, not made into a spoken-claim card because it was not clearly a qualifying speech, press conference, reporter press byte or interview. [Indian Express, police notice](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/delhi-police-notice-cjp-abhijeet-dipke-lehra-do-tiranga-connaught-place-march-10924493/). Notice text is the authorities' position; a signed judicial judgment on merits was not reviewed.
2. **At approximately 16:50** police detained Dipke as he stepped out for the march. Neha Bora of AISA was independently detained around Mandi House. Their custody, identities and organisations are distinct. [Indian Express, detention observations](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/abhijeet-dipke-detained-delhi-cops-removing-cjp-protesters-connaught-place-10924641/); [Indian Express, late report](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/cjp-abhijeet-dipke-neha-bora-detained-connaught-place-protest-delhi-police-10924983/).
3. **Approximately 17:00–19:00**, protesters with national flags occupied streets around Central Park and Palika Bazaar, but the park itself remained sealed. Original site and full proposed route completion are **not** established. The Indian Express photographed and interviewed participants around the barriers. Its first field dispatch estimated about **1,000** and a later synthesis around **2,000**; a [Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/security-stepped-up-indias-capital-cockroach-movement-rally-2026-10-16/) described nearly **500** gathered in parts of Connaught Place. These may reflect different locations and times; none is a certified count. [Early reporter-led interviews](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/cjp-tiranga-march-connaught-place-palika-bazaar-protesters-gyanesh-kumar-manipur-10924699/); [late Indian Express report](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/cjp-abhijeet-dipke-neha-bora-detained-connaught-place-protest-delhi-police-10924983/).
4. **Detentions**: Indian Express quoted a police account of **more than 100**, while other PTI/Delhi Police reporting used **around 150**. A provisional detention is not a criminal conviction; detailed person-level records, notices, legal grounds and releases are missing. [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/cjp-abhijeet-dipke-neha-bora-detained-connaught-place-protest-delhi-police-10924983/); [Mint, published 17 October 02:42 IST](https://www.livemint.com/news/india/tiranga-march-in-delhi-cjp-chief-abhijeet-dipke-s-whereabouts-unknown-neha-bora-located-at-rk-puram-police-station-11792183587644.html).
5. **Overnight** AISA said Bora was traced to RK Puram Police Station and reported obstacles to lawyer access and medical accommodation, while CJP sought a confirmed location and procedural status for Dipke. This is **as-of-publication sourcing**, not proof of an enforced disappearance or that either individual was released. Obtain all custody registers, transfer logs and judicial records before an unqualified legal verdict.

## Courts: do not mix unlike decisions

The **15 October Delhi High Court** declined an injunction requested by a vendor against the prospective Connaught Place gathering and recorded the police assurance to regulate it. That was not a licence to enter the closed park or a judgment on the validity of preventive detention. On **16 October**, the **Supreme Court declined an urgent hearing** concerning NDMC's two-day Central Park closure. This was no final merits judgment either. The operative orders are priority evidence requests. [Indian Express on SC refusal](https://indianexpress.com/article/legal-news/supreme-court-ndmc-central-park-closure-cjp-protest-cockroach-janta-party-abhijeet-dipke-10924091/); [15 October High Court report](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Oct/15/delhi-hc-refuses-to-restrain-cjps-protest-at-connaught-place-on-friday).

Both the traders' access concerns and peaceful assembly rights deserve documentary treatment without claiming the courts completely endorsed either side. The November 3 High Court listing remains prospective.

Public endorsement is not a remittance to CJP, nor evidence of AEI instruction, foreign government funding, or a role in CJP executive decisions. [Moneycontrol / Amitava Kumar's reported account](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/india/salman-rushdie-hails-cjp-protesters-mira-nair-calls-herself-cockroach-mom-14053847.html).
