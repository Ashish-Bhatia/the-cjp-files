# The CJP Files: 15 October 2026 evening field investigation

Evidence cutoff: **15 October 2026, 19:11 IST**. Release: 15 October 2026, evening. This is a source-limited investigation of publicly reported speech and observed proceedings, not an exhaustive transcription archive.

## Main finding: two distinct public student coalition events

- **7 October:** A CJP–AISA–AISF–SFI press briefing and reported ASAP presence had already been recorded. [Earlier source](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/cjp-student-groups-call-for-nationwide-mobilisation-ahead-of-october-10-protest-against-cec-gyanesh-kumar-video).
- **15 October:** A new photographed briefing at the Press Club of India placed CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke publicly alongside **AISA leader Neha Bora, SFI figure Aishe Ghosh, an AISF representative identified as Viraaj, advocate Prashant Bhushan and actor Prakash Raj**. The Indian Express also attributed spoken interventions to Kapil Sibal and Sheikh Salauddin. It reported a joint CJP–AISA appeal to participate in a prospective October 16 tricolour programme. [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/cjp-abhijeet-dipke-aisa-neha-bora-connaught-place-protest-electoral-roll-sir-gyanesh-kumar-10923289/); [The Week/PTI event image](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2026/10/15/ahead-of-cjp-s-tiranga-march-on-friday-chaos-ensues-at-abhijeet-dipke-neha-bora-presser.html).

**Inference supported:** public, issue-specific alignment and a common protest invitation. **Inference not supported:** one binding committee, shared finances, undocumented AAP direction, AEI sponsorship, criminal conspiracy, or a single person issuing all organisations' instructions. A photograph proves attendance at a particular event; reported quotations establish public statements subject to full-recording review.

## New court and transport record

**15 October Delhi High Court:** A two-judge bench declined to issue the requested express injunction against the **proposed** CJP action at Connaught Place, took the police undertaking to take lawful regulation measures on record, and adjourned the petition to **3 November**. This is no finding that October 16 protest is legally approved without conditions, and no record that it happened. The petitioner, a vendor, raised commercial disruption and access concerns. [New Indian Express/PTI](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Oct/15/delhi-hc-refuses-to-restrain-cjps-protest-at-connaught-place-on-friday).

**14 October DMRC affidavit, reported 15 October:** DMRC says **271 trains completed 4,157 scheduled trips** on October 10. It reports **45 of 303 stations** with police-directed entry/exit restrictions, after an initial 57-station proposal, and a later **six-station** restriction on October 12. DMRC stated October 10 ridership was around **32 lakh**, versus approximately **58 lakh** on a normal comparable Saturday. Those are party-filed statements to the Supreme Court, not its final factual findings. [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/metro-curbs-police-dmrc-supreme-court-10922183/).

**Press-briefing disturbance:** The Week reports shouting during the 15 October briefing. Without original video and independent attribution, no claim is made about the identity, organisation or motive of the interrupters. [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2026/10/15/ahead-of-cjp-s-tiranga-march-on-friday-chaos-ensues-at-abhijeet-dipke-neha-bora-presser.html).

## Separating political connections

- **CJP–AISA, AISF and SFI:** public joint appearance in both 7 and 15 October briefing records, with named participants on October 15. This supports two specific open coordination episodes.
- **Bhushan:** a recorded supportive May interview, August public commentary, reported 10 October march attendance, 11 October Kolkata conference, and the latest 15 October press appearance. His declaration of a regime-change objective is a public political position. None of these documents authority over CJP accounts or employment.
- **Yogendra Yadav:** reported 10 October participation and 15 October edited interview advocating future synchronisation without a central command. He was expelled from AAP in 2015; current AAP command cannot be inferred from historical affiliations.
- **AAP:** named historical relationships and October public endorsement/ASAP participation are evidence of specific contact types. They do not establish a shared budget or operational instructions.
- **Congress:** earlier reported invitation outreach is not a verified accepted alliance. A separate report describes possible Congress–AISA visibility tensions, but relies partly on anonymous sources and is **context**, not proof of control. [Jansatta 15 October](https://www.jansatta.com/national/neha-bora-rahul-and-priyanka-gandhi-congress-nsui-controversy/4746192/).

This is an evidence-taxonomy comparison; matching common peaceful protest tactics does **not** prove implementation of an AEI manual.

The existing **15 October forecast for 16 October** remains Sharp **#18, Displays of flags and symbolic colours**, a **conditional repeat** because speakers again invited a tricolour gathering. **#47, Assemblies of protest or support** remains an alternative if a gathering takes place without verified flags. Neither future method is counted as having occurred. If organisers cancel, police prevent attendance, participants use a different format, or no independently verified observation emerges, the forecast fails or stays unresolved. No reliable prediction of a previously unseen Sharp method is supported.
