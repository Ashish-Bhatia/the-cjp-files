# Investigation 18 October 2026 | Gene Sharp, Otpor, Popović, CANVAS and CJP

**Status:** source-attributed public-record comparison, prepared 18 October 2026 IST. Not a certification of completeness. This memorandum distinguishes original scholarly/organisational material, reporting of public oral statements, and observed events. No covert-coordination allegation is adopted without primary operational evidence.

## 1. Principal finding

**Gene Sharp → Otpor → CANVAS is a historically evidenced transmission of ideas and personnel. CANVAS → Cockroach Janta Party is NOT documented in the sources reviewed.** The last arrow is an untested potential influence, not a finding. Similarity to public nonviolent-struggle playbooks is expected even when people have not read them. Indian satyagraha, student politics and ordinary constitutional protest provide competing domestic explanations.

## 2. Read the chronology of ideas accurately

- 1973: Gene Sharp, *The Politics of Nonviolent Action*, in three parts: *Power and Struggle*, *The Methods of Nonviolent Action* (the 198-method list), and *The Dynamics of Nonviolent Action*. Original catalog: https://www.aeinstein.org/198-methods-of-nonviolent-action . Publishing context: https://www.nonviolent-conflict.org/resource/the-politics-of-nonviolent-action-volume-2/ .
- 1993: Sharp's *From Dictatorship to Democracy* was originally published for the Burmese democratic struggle. Later editions and dissemination do not by themselves identify a given reader or student. https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/From_Dictatorship_to_Democracy ; https://www.aeinstein.org/digital-library .
- 1998–2000: Serbian Otpor formed and mobilised before the appearance of the 2007 CANVAS training curriculum. Crucial nuance: Popović and Slobodan Đinović said **they first encountered Sharp's work in late April 2000**, when Robert Helvey brought books. Their account confirms influence during an existing movement, not that Sharp invented all initial Otpor activity. https://canvasopedia.org/project/washington-post-gene-sharp-passed-away-ideas-will-go-inspiring-activists-around-world/ .
- 2007: CANVAS *Core Curriculum: A Guide to Effective Nonviolent Struggle*, copyright/published by CANVAS (PDF pp. 2–5), includes pillars of support, communication, movement management, nonviolent discipline and dilemma actions. https://canvasopedia.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/CANVAS-Core-Curriculum_EN-compressed_compressed-compressed.pdf .
- 2015: Srđa Popović with Matthew Miller, *Blueprint for Revolution* (Penguin Random House book page). Publisher describes humour, small achievable campaigns, coalition building, and making repression backfire. Do not pretend publisher copy equals an independently authenticated line-by-line reading of the entire copyrighted book. https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/228578/blueprint-for-revolution-by-srdja-popovic/ .
- Related CANVAS primary-source library: *50 Crucial Points*, *Making Oppression Backfire*, *A Brief History of OTPOR*, *Pranksters vs Autocrats*. The original publications page outlines their scope. https://canvasopedia.org/publications/ .

## 4. Dated public words: the 'regime change' dispute

At the **15 October 2026 Press Club event**, ThePrint reports senior advocate Prashant Bhushan saying: "We all want regime change, getting regime change is every citizen's constitutional right" and arguing that peaceful disaffection was not in itself a criminal offence. ThePrint reported participants and their separate comments. **This is Bhushan's public political statement, not independent evidence that CJP adopted his objective**, and a constitutional claim quoted at a briefing is not a court judgment. https://theprint.in/politics/at-dipke-neha-bora-sibal-press-conference-regime-change-gyanesh-modi-silent-erasure-in-sir/3072888/ .

On **16 October**, The Tribune reported CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke explicitly distancing CJP's demand to remove the CEC from a call to change the government. Treat as his stated position, not proof of every private motive. https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/dipke-clarifies-cjps-protest-goals-distances-himself-from-prashant-bhushan-neha-bora/amp .

BJP figures, including Rajnath Singh and Devendra Fadnavis, publicly alleged an opposition-driven regime-change agenda. These are attributed claims, not documentary proof of CANVAS training, foreign finance or covert coordination. https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/pune/devendra-fadnavis-opposition-regime-change-sir-cjp-protests-10925780/ . Independent party leaders and their individual comments must not be flattened into a single organisational position.

## 7. Direct-link evidence test

The investigation requires one or more of: authenticated training rosters or participant identification; contemporary dated acknowledgments citing Popović/CANVAS/Sharp; verifiable financial entries identifying source and recipient; original communications describing tactical assistance; contract/grant/workshop records with participant and date; or corroborated firsthand testimony. None was established in reviewed evidence for **CANVAS → CJP**. Do not replace missing proof with recycled logo similarity, a foreign appearance, funding conjecture or press-conference proximity.

The absence of evidence after an open-source screen is **unresolved**, not a definitive statement that contact never occurred. No individual should be accused of unlawful conspiracy on these grounds.
