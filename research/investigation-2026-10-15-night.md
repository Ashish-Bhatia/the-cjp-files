## Electoral-roll investigation: claims versus mechanism

A count of names absent from a **draft** post-SIR list is not automatically a count of permanently removed eligible voters. Separate four denominators: pre-revision entries; draft entries; final verified eligible entries after enrolments/restorations; and verified eligible adults entitled to enrol. Comparisons to total adult population require allowance for nationality, duplicates, place of residence and eligibility criteria.

The Election Commission's [Form 7 instructions](https://voters.eci.gov.in/guidelines/Form-7_en.pdf) and [public FAQ](https://www.eci.gov.in/faq/2/9) describe the formal objections/deletion application framework, including reasons and evidentiary burdens. Because 15 October reporting describes further guidance, any specific new deletion rule must be checked against the *dated* 2026 circular before treating older guidance as exhaustive. The available published Form 7 is an objection mechanism, not by itself proof of ethnic targeting. A robust test needs redacted application logs, disposal decisions, hearings, grounds, constituency-level attrition and ECI answers, as well as independent voter accounts.

The 15 October SSR timetable creates observable upcoming reconciliation points. It is an opportunity to test allegations, not proof that all objections will be cured.

## Police and court: separate disputed acts from findings

The Delhi High Court's decision **not to issue an anticipatory restraint** on the proposed 16 October Connaught Place gathering is not a blanket judicial authorisation of any conduct at any site. It recorded the police position on lawful regulation and deferred proceedings. The substantive legality of orders, custody, emergency restrictions and alleged contempt is not finally decided by newspaper summaries.

[Indian Express on DMRC's affidavit](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/metro-curbs-police-dmrc-supreme-court-10922183/) reports 4,157 scheduled trips operated on 10 October, with police-directed entry and exit limits at 45 of 303 stations. The operator's sworn submission is weightier than a slogan that all train services stopped, but remains a party-filed account. The Union explanation about apologies also does not resolve the contempt dispute. Individual allegations about cameras, arrests or mistreatment require original footage, treatment records and the court's operative text.

## AEI/Gene Sharp: exhaustive-number taxonomy, limited causal inference

[AEI's published list](https://www.aeinstein.org/198-methods-of-nonviolent-action) has **198** numbered methods. The research register checks all 198 and, at this cutoff, classifies **15 documented, 16 possible, 1 announced and 166 not established**; the separately dated occurrence ledger contains **22** entries. The latest 15 October press event repeats **#1, Public Speeches**. The newly reviewed 13 October interview is a reported interview about prior activity, not evidence of a fresh protest action. Yadav's anecdote about a barricade is not sufficient to establish a newly adopted method by CJP. No new numbered method is warranted here.

The method taxonomy describes actions common to many movements. A behavioural resemblance or the fact that Gene Sharp wrote publicly about civil resistance **does not prove** CJP read, adopted, was instructed by or was financed by AEI. A causation case would require dated original training, instructions, contracts, correspondence voluntarily disclosed, public funding records or comparable auditable first-party evidence. None is established in the reviewed corpus.
