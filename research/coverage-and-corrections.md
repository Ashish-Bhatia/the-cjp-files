# Coverage, evidence and corrections — 15 October 2026

## Publication rule added 15 October 2026

The public-facing claim review is restricted to attributed statements made in **speeches, press conferences, press bytes / reporter interactions, and interviews**. A single online post, CJP-branded website, unauthenticated manifesto, anonymous briefing, private correspondence, and a reporter's own inference **cannot originate a claim card**. Court decisions, law, public institutional releases, statistics and press reporting on independently observed events remain admissible as **verification material**, with source type marked.

The prior 26-case register is superseded by **22 specifically sourced public-statement reviews**. This removal narrows source eligibility, not the underlying existence of topics. Every review identifies speaker, reporting date, eligible format, paraphrased proposition, document-based comparison, outstanding records and link. English summaries of Hindi and Marathi statements are editorial paraphrases, not verbatim translations.

### Findings that need to stay qualified

- Dipke's denial of party direction does not establish audited financial or governance independence; the converse allegation of AAP control requires specific operational evidence.
- Cross-border or AEI claims are attributed to speakers when made in eligible settings. No original evidence of AEI–CJP instruction, training or financial transfer has been independently verified here. Similar protest tactics alone cannot prove direction.
- A reported national-scale electoral-roll change is not equivalent to wrongful permanent deletion of that number of eligible voters. Definitions and final rolls matter.
- July talks, the 25 July resignation and the 1 September court settlement are distinct milestones. A speaker's prediction of blanket FIR relief must be tested against the text of the signed order and any exceptions.
- October custody is reported, but the authenticated magistrate order and police undertakings are still required before making any legal conclusion about the alleged breach.
