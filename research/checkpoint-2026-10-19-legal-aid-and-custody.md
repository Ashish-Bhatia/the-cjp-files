## Evidence-to-conclusion policy

1. A public announcement is established as an announcement. It is **not** synonymous with a verified completed transfer.
2. Legal aid for an arrested protester is analytically distinct from financing transport, media, stage rental, campaign operations or unlawful conduct.
3. An NGO advocacy statement is not a court order; an FIR or non-cognisable report is not a finding of culpability.
4. A peace-and-good-behaviour bond is not automatically a blanket prohibition on constitutionally protected peaceful protest. Verified bond language is necessary for a ruling-specific conclusion.
5. Images and videos require an original capture, provenance, timing, location, hash and comparison to court/police originals before independent forensic certification.
6. Nothing added here substantiates a foreign donor, an AEI/CANVAS operational relationship, or an illicit regime-change operation.
