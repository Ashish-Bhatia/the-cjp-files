# Investigation supplement: CJP public-claim and association audit
**Evidence cutoff:** 15 October 2026, India Standard Time. **Edition:** contemporaneous follow-on to the May–October chronology.
**Scope:** Interviews, publicly spoken press conferences, speeches, and attributable reporter interactions produce claim cards. Court judgments, police explanations, written petitions, social posts and news reporting without a spoken claim serve only as testing context or dated events.

## Executive findings
1. **Foreign direction is still not evidenced.** BJP president Nitin Nabin alleged on 10 October that CJP-adjacent opposition activities were linked to unnamed foreign actors, but a video-reported press conference provides no attributable foreign operator, AEI correspondent, transaction, training record or instructions. A separate **24 July MEA briefing** was repeatedly misrepresented: spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal stated only that he had no information to share. MEA specifically corrected a viral post falsely portraying this as confirmation of *no* foreign funding. Neither side's public claims substitute for financing records.
2. **The gurdwara confrontation has competing allegations and different actors.** On 11 October Saurav Das said police entered Gurdwara Bangla Sahib with weapons/batons, removed protesters and pressured staff. Separately, the Delhi Sikh Gurdwara Management Committee alleged disturbance by **AAP-linked** individuals; police also provided a counter-explanation of footage. Those accounts cannot be merged into a proven CJP-directed operation or a judicial finding of unlawful police conduct. Preserve allegation, counter-position, geography, footage gaps, and chronology.
3. **An apparent misattribution matters.** In one Tribune story, the nine-hour detention account is confusingly ascribed to “Ranka” using female pronouns. The News Minute explicitly attributes this first-person account to **Ratna Singh**, the CJP legal-affairs lead. Her October 10 application to Saket court was dismissed on procedural shortcomings; the dismissal did not decide whether her alleged confinement occurred or was lawful.
4. **Public solidarity is not structural membership.** Dhruv Rathee disavowed joining or being formally affiliated with CJP in a publicly available video (published 12 October). TMC MP Mahua Moitra described distinct opposition protest schedules while supporting protest rights in an October 14 interview. India Today identified Rohit Patel as a CJP West Zone coordinator in a cross-party panel. Those are three different relationship types: ally, political supporter, and reported officeholder.
5. **Public-order justifications require their own evidence.** BJP spokesperson Ajay Alok defended restricting prohibited demonstration venues in an October 14 televised debate. The legality of de-notifying Jantar Mantar, police enforcement, remand orders and transport restrictions must be evaluated against notifications, petitions, judicial orders, and both police and protesters' accounts. Protest restrictions are not alone a constitutional declaration of Emergency.
6. **The Sharp method register remains a taxonomy, not a theory of causation.** A 14 October reporter interaction by Dipke and the Patel television panel are repeat examples of method **#1, Public Speeches**. The full 198-method audit remains **15 documented / 16 possible / 1 announced / 166 not established**. Repetition is not new tactical discovery, and an AEI connection remains unproven.

## External allegations that are NOT qualifying spoken-claim entries
**Mary Millben, 14 October:** The newspaper report traces an assertion about US donors to a written X post. No authenticated finance trail or named donor is provided. A published post is not a public *speech, interview, press conference or press byte* within the adopted source restriction, therefore it appears only in the context timeline and source library. Do not reframe it as an established US–CJP or AEI link.

**DSGMC police complaint:** Its reporting concerns AAP-associated protesters allegedly inside Bangla Sahib. Even where the movement's cause overlaps, the complaint is not evidence that CJP instructed those individuals. Do not treat religious complaints as adjudicated facts.
