# Investigation update: outreach, contested policing and regional protest records
**Evidence cutoff:** 15 October 2026, approximately midday IST. **Editorial rule:** Only attributed spoken material from speeches, press conferences, press bytes and interviews creates claim-review entries. Written court filings, permit orders, social posts and reported attempted contacts are context or chronology, not new spoken claims.

## What changed

### 1. The Congress–CJP relationship is still not reducible to 'one command'
- **12 October, public press conference:** Rahul Gandhi described anti-CEC mobilisations as one protest when asked about students and civil-society actions. His assertion of common purpose is documented as his political position, not proof that CJP had joined Congress or the INDIA bloc.
- **13 October, outreach reported on 15 October:** An Indian Express investigation reports an attempted Congress invitation to Abhijeet Dipke, reportedly using lawyer Prashant Bhushan as intermediary. Sources differed on contact and enthusiasm, and senior CJP members were in custody. This is neither a verified written invitation nor evidence of acceptance, meeting attendance, financing, party membership or control.
- **14 October, Dipke:** Attributed press remarks describing organisational independence remain in the earlier register. The new outreach report is evidence to test against, not a reason to label all solidarity as a structural link.
- **Analytical finding:** Political solidarity, invitations, former membership, protest participation, coordinated actions and current organisational control have six distinct evidentiary thresholds. Do not collapse one into another.

### 2. Goa adds a separate, local protest strand, not an assumed national instruction chain
- **7 October:** Organisers reportedly applied for permission in Panaji.
- **9 October:** North Goa authorities reportedly rejected permission for the 11 October Azad Maidan gathering. Police cited past conduct and a placard carried by one person during an earlier protest. A placard displayed by a participant is not automatically a CJP endorsement or a view shared by an entire protest movement.
- **11 October:** Seven people at a Goa CJP-linked gathering were reportedly detained. Following a habeas plea, a court reportedly directed their release that evening. Reporting paraphrases the judge's concern over the legality of custody, but the signed order is outstanding.
- **Classification:** Regional CJP-affiliated activity with uncertain authority chain. This event should be considered in method #47 only after the precise act and organisational affiliation are established; it is not a new, demonstrated Gene Sharp method on present evidence.

### 3. 'Arrested only for displaying the flag' is an incomplete legal account
- **14 October, Ajinkya Shinde (public reporter interaction):** Stated the group merely displayed the flag and voiced a patriotic slogan. This is first-person testimony about their intended action.
- **Police position:** Reported accusations also include entry through private premises, prohibited-site restrictions, barricades and alleged breach of peace bonds. These must be evaluated against authenticated video, site notification, remand report and original bonds. Allegations and custody are **not** findings of criminal guilt.
- **14 October, advocate Abhisht Hela (public reporter interaction):** Criticised the executive magistrate decision, alleged inadequate application of judicial mind and questioned the procedure. His public legal opinion is included, but has not been adopted as a judicial determination.
- **Separate issues:** Whether flag-waving is lawful, whether other conduct occurred, whether a lawful order was breached, whether preventive detention was authorised, and whether five days of custody were legally sustainable must be assessed independently.

### 4. Police defence and internet-curb evidence has become more specific
Indian Express reported Delhi Police Commissioner Anurag Kumar's affidavit contesting contempt allegations and defending 10 October preventive steps based on intelligence and previous disturbances. Police said people removed from sites were temporarily held in makeshift centres for two to six hours. This institutional account conflicts with allegations by some detainees and is **not independently validated**.

Union Home Secretary Govind Mohan reportedly tendered an unconditional apology while asserting that a time-limited cellular internet shutdown was a proportionate public-order action. The affidavit specifies **9 October, 22:00 to 10 October, 22:00 IST**, within **four kilometres of Raisina Hill**, for **cellular internet**, distinguishing it from fixed broadband/Wi-Fi. These are the government's filed assertions. Obtain the actual order, telecom logs, operator records and Supreme Court findings before adopting exact technical impact as independently verified.

### 5. Prospective protest: court listing is not a ban
On 15 October the Delhi High Court reportedly agreed to hear a petition seeking to prevent the 16 October Connaught Place demonstration. The court's agreement to hear a petition is **not** evidence it granted interim restraint. The demonstration is future activity at this cutoff.
