# The coalition question: Yadav, Bhushan, Left student organisations and AAP

Evidence cut-off: 15 October 2026, IST. Research release: 15 October, afternoon.

## Method and conclusion

This investigation separates six evidentiary categories: publicly expressed support, physical participation, recorded joint mobilisation, prior affiliation, reported intermediary outreach, and present operational control. A shared venue or protest demand is insufficient to prove a financing arrangement or instruction chain. Only speeches, press conferences, press interactions and interviews enter the public spoken-claim register. Reported attendance, NGO allegations, intermediary reports and court filings are context only.

**Core finding:** Public issue-specific joint mobilisation is documented, chiefly the 7 October CJP–AISA–AISF–SFI press conference (with reported participation of ASAP, AAP's student wing). Former AAP links and high-profile public endorsements are also documented. Present centralized control by AAP, the Left, Congress, Yogendra Yadav, Prashant Bhushan or AEI has not been established from the reviewed sources.

## Historical context: Yadav, Bhushan and AAP, 2015–2016

- **4 March 2015:** Founding AAP leaders Yadav and Bhushan removed from AAP Political Affairs Committee amid public internal conflict, according to contemporaneous reporting: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/yogendra-yadav-prashant-bhushan-out-of-aaps-pac-arvind-kejriwals-resignation-rejected/articleshow/46458786.cms
- **28 March 2015:** AAP National Council removed them from the National Executive. Report: https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/live-aaps-national-council-meeting-set-to-begin-amid-high-police-presence/
- **20 April 2015:** AAP disciplinary committee expelled Yadav and Bhushan from the party. The party's contemporaneous notice: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/aaps-news-release-on-expulsion-of-rebels/articleshow/46993281.cms. The competing faction's allegations must be attributed, not adopted.
- **2 October 2016:** Yadav and Bhushan launched Swaraj India, separate from AAP, after Swaraj Abhiyan. https://indianexpress.com/article/india/india-news-india/prashant-bhushan-yogendra-yadav-now-form-new-swaraj-india-party-3061467/
- This chronology establishes **historical leadership followed by organisational separation**. It neither establishes nor rules out a fresh 2026 AAP contact and must not be used to attribute AAP's 2026 decisions to Yadav or Bhushan.

## Organisation-specific findings

### Yogendra Yadav
- The 21 May Satya Hindi interview video is an attributable public-interview lead, but its full transcript and timecoded original wording were not authenticated and it does not add a spoken claim.
- Reported march participation with Bhushan, Neha Bora and Dipankar Bhattacharya on 10 October is relevant physical action.
- His former AAP leadership and April 2015 expulsion, followed by involvement in separate Swaraj formations, are documented; this does not place him inside AAP in 2026.
- The 15 October edited The Federal interview attributes to him a prospective coordination proposal. He distinctly rejects treating separate campaigns as a single centralized-command body.
- His assertions that earlier actions developed without planning and that many attendees were non-party volunteers are participant observations, not comprehensive call-record or attendance audits.

### Prashant Bhushan
- Reuters' 28 May recorded soundbites establish encouragement to organise a careful offline movement. The rhetoric resembles broad nonviolent-mobilisation advice but is not a documented AEI or CJP chain of command.
- His founding AAP association ended in the documented April 2015 expulsion, followed by a separate Swaraj Abhiyan/Swaraj India history.
- A 4 August reported talk expresses support and qualitative optimism.
- On 11 October in Kolkata, Bhushan made further public remarks on institutional capture, police accountability and protest, reviewed as PC-093–094.
- The 13 October attempted Congress invitation via Bhushan is anonymously sourced reporting published 15 October. It is not an eligible original spoken Bhushan claim; no written invitation, acceptance or mandate is established.

### Left and other student organisations
- AISF book-stall participants were reported at Jantar Mantar on 20 July (PUCL Delhi statement); rights-group allegations about police action are not independently adjudicated.
- SFI and DYFI protests in Chennai on 22 July are separately reported allied solidarity events, not proof of CJP orders.
- CJP spokesperson Saurav Das was reported on 1 August as thanking AISF and SFI for organisational help. Only a short excerpt is available and the original speech date is unknown.
- The 7 October press conference is joint, attributable public campaigning. AISA president Neha Bora's remarks are counted; opposition unity claims do not imply organisational merger.
- AISF-associated activists travelling from Pune were reported held at Itarsi en route to 10 October protests. Police documentation and any final judicial findings are outstanding.

### Aam Aadmi Party
- ThePrint's 13 August reporting identifies six people within 12 CJP officeholders with **different** types of past AAP link, including service, volunteering and public support. Do not translate all six into former officeholders.
- Dipke's June interview, AAP spokesperson Sanjeev Jha's August remarks and Bhagwant Mann's 8 October public-event comments acknowledge the founder's past role while denying a current instruction relationship. The denials are attributed testimony, not independently inspected finance or telecom data.
- ASAP, the AAP student wing, was reported attending the 7 October joint student briefing. Kejriwal issued an appeal for the CJP protest on 9 October.
- AAP/CJP operational control, current party membership and funding are not established on this file.

## Myth-vs-reality tests
| Allegation or inference | Limited finding |
|---|---|
| 'The Left fronts had no hand in mobilisation' | Too categorical. CJP-linked speaker acknowledgement plus joint 7 Oct press conference supports participation and coordination. |
| 'The Left secretly owns CJP' | Not established. Acknowledged co-mobilisation neither authenticates hidden control nor financing. |
| 'CJP is demonstrably an AAP front' | Not established. Historical AAP connections and 2026 solidarity do not demonstrate AAP's current authority. |
| 'AAP had no history with CJP organisers' | Contradicted by on-record and reporting evidence of earlier AAP service/volunteering. |
| 'Bhushan advised street protest, therefore he commands it' | Not established. Advice is publicly documented, but no instructions or role appointment were verified. |
| 'Yadav said all protests follow one headquarters' | Contradicted by his October 15 interview distinguishing separate strands and opposing a single command model. |
| 'Joint student press conference proves foreign/AEI orchestration' | Not established. Method similarity and coalitions lack an evidenced cross-border financial or organisational chain. |
