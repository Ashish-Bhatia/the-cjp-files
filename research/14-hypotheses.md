# Volume 14. Competing hypotheses
## A provisional evidence-testing matrix

**Evidence cutoff:** 19 October 2026  
**Scope:** Foundational data only. No conclusion about secret direction or finances is possible on present records.

This chapter tests explanations for the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) as hypotheses, not labels. A person or organisation must never be assigned clandestine motive solely from prior affiliation, shared protest slogans, or acquaintance with other demonstrators.

### Evidence grading

**Documented fact** means the original or a reliable contemporary report has established a narrow event such as a public appointment, press conference or announced policy. **Attributed claim** means a named actor spoke, rather than that the content is true. **Allegation** means the claimed conduct has not passed relevant independent or legal tests. **Analyst inference** must be labelled and able to survive plausible alternatives.

A full causal explanation requires more than confirming a source exists. Relevant evidence must establish the proposed mechanism, its chronology and its unique explanatory value against rival explanations.

### AAP affiliation versus contemporary control

The first material historical association is Dipke's reported involvement with AAP from 2020 to 2022. That supports a narrower proposition, **a prominent CJP founder previously had relevant political experience**. It cannot support the stronger proposition, **AAP directed the May 2026 founding**, without a mechanism connecting the time periods. Other individuals' past political experiences require individual primary verification and must not be combined into a generic conspiracy map.

**References:** [The Indian Express, May CJP explainer](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/cockroach-janta-party-founder-motive-bjp-congress-details-10705858/); [The Indian Express, original interview](https://indianexpress.com/article/political-pulse/cockroach-janta-party-threats-family-hounded-opinion-10703302/).

### Congress outreach versus coalition membership

The Indian Express reported that Congress representatives attempted to reach Dipke in mid-October to invite him to a meeting. It also described CJP as separate from the INDIA bloc's **12 October** march. This is a useful counterexample to simple graph-proximity inference: two sides may publicly favour similar electoral demands while preserving independent organising decisions.

**Reference:** [The Indian Express, 15 October](https://indianexpress.com/article/political-pulse/cockroach-janta-party-cjp-congress-abhijeet-dipke-rahul-gandhi-high-tea-10922245/).

### The foreign-influence allegation

The May explainer attributed foreign-influence allegations to BJP figures and documented criticism from other political actors. No original receipt, international donor, signed service agreement, public admission of operational training, or money trail has been verified in this edition. The correct evaluation is **unsupported in the reviewed records, further documentary investigation required**, not a finding that hidden contact exists or is impossible.

**Reference:** [The Indian Express, 24 May](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/cockroach-janta-party-founder-motive-bjp-congress-details-10705858/).

### Decision rule for updating an assessment

Upgrade a hypothesis only after the new source (a) confirms the stated mechanism rather than merely co-presence; (b) fits the actual event dates; (c) survives genuine independent corroboration; and (d) is assessed against contrary documents. Record the previous hypothesis version, source ID and justification in Git history and the corrections register.
