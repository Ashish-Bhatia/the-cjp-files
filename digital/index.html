Inside the social-media battle

Satirical origins, public campaign calls and alleged fabricated clips are distinct phenomena requiring distinct evidence.

From social identity to street call

A press profile dated the movement's public internet emergence to 16 May and reported restriction of its original X account around 21 May. Neither the account's native creation record nor the precise platform restriction request has been retained in this edition. Such missing primary records are crucial for a defensible digital-origin timeline. S43↗ S44↗

Reuters traced the early CJP identity to an online reaction following the Supreme Court remark. The independent June 6 Jantar Mantar turnout confirms that the digital idea had become an offline event. The later sequence of public announcements and rallies shows social platforms helped distribute practical instructions. None of this, on its own, establishes paid automation or platform collusion. S35↗ S02↗ S30↗

An account reposting a common slogan or graphic can reflect voluntary solidarity. For a covert-coordination claim, investigators need attributable account controls, timed source posts, identical assets with provenance, unusually synchronous amplification and alternative explanations such as a publicly published event toolkit.

October: claims of fake video

After the 16 October rally, Indian Express reported that Delhi Police registered FIRs related to alleged fabricated footage and took action concerning social-media accounts. The underlying videos, acquisition metadata, originals and independent forensic assessment have not been obtained. It would be premature to identify any unnamed account as a bot or to call a particular clip AI-generated. S19↗

Platform compliance with police requests, account suspension, algorithmic promotion and corporate intent are separate questions. Public evidence that a clip circulated or that a request was issued does not prove deliberate platform participation. An audited media file will eventually need an earliest posting, chronology, location cues, original frames, context and recorded uncertainty.

No fictional metrics

This site does not present follower-growth charts, a bot percentage or a viral reach number. Without reproducible exports and a consistent sampling frame, they would be false precision. The chronology remains searchable by event rather than masquerading as a complete platform dataset.
