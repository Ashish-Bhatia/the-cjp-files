The central distinction

History is not
an instruction chain.

The earliest records here predate CJP by twelve years. They concern different parties, institutions, campaigns and professional careers. A known past connection is relevant context, but does not establish secret present-day coordination.

Verified past

Former affiliations

Who worked for whom, when they did so, and whether the relationship had ended.

Visible present

Public cooperation

Who appeared, signed a public call, spoke jointly or separately, and on what dated occasion.

Not proved here

External control

Training, instructions, payments and a foreign command structure require direct corroborating records.

03 / Six comparative case studies

Similar methods.
Different histories.

Each country is examined for a dated protest history, an observable parallel, a critical difference and proof of any direct organisational link. Country-level similarities are analytical comparisons, not evidence of a transnational operation.

Bangladesh 2024 / student quota movement

Documented history

University students initially disputed a public-employment quota. Protest demands widened amid state violence; Sheikh Hasina resigned on 5 August. A February 2025 UN investigation documented grave alleged abuses.

What resembles CJP

A sectoral student grievance developed into a broader national political demand; online expression and a state crackdown shaped the public response.

What does not establish a link

Major differences concern intensity and fatality of violence, constitutional context and a completed change of government. Dipke publicly rejected the parallel in August 2026.

Inspect original reporting or primary text ↗
Nepal 2025 / Gen Z protests

Documented history

A ban on social-media platforms sparked demonstrations already motivated by anger at perceived corruption and a shortage of opportunity. Prime Minister K. P. Sharma Oli resigned in September after deadly unrest.

What resembles CJP

Youth identity, online network mobilisation, rapid spread beyond an initial grievance.

What does not establish a link

Police use of force, deaths, institutional dynamics and government resignation make it an imperfect analogue. Dipke publicly disavowed the comparison.

Inspect original reporting or primary text ↗
France 2018 Yellow Vests; October 2026 student and union protests

Documented history

The 2018 Yellow Vests emerged over fuel prices, adopted visible clothing symbolism and developed broader economic grievances. October 2026 protests brought students, unions and remaining Yellow Vest activists into public view.

What resembles CJP

Visible symbols, coalition growth, repeated street assemblies and friction around policing.

What does not establish a link

Organisers, core grievances and violent incidents differ. Similar visibility tactics provide no evidence of shared CJP personnel or funding.

Inspect original reporting or primary text ↗
Italy 2019 Sardines; October 2026 Milan unrest

Documented history

The Sardines movement arose in Bologna against Matteo Salvini, using a fish symbol, flash gatherings and an initially non-party identity. Separately, Milan saw October 2026 cost-of-living demonstrations and clashes.

What resembles CJP

Satirical identity, unusual animal branding and efforts to bring politically frustrated people into public gatherings.

What does not establish a link

Sardines and Milan's 2026 protests are separate phenomena. Shared aesthetic language does not establish common design, sponsors or command.

Inspect original reporting or primary text ↗
Albania 2026 / Flamingo campaign and October student protest

Documented history

June demonstrations over a coastal development used flamingo placards, with some participants demanding wider governmental reforms. By October, separate or expanding student-led protests over education and corruption involved serious clashes.

What resembles CJP

Animal imagery, amplification of a focused grievance and increasingly broad political demands.

What does not establish a link

Escalation and violence diverge from the nonviolent claims of CJP leaders. No primary material examined establishes a CJP–Albanian organiser relationship.

Inspect original reporting or primary text ↗
Serbia 1998–2000 Otpor; CANVAS curriculum; 2024–26 student protests

Documented history

Otpor helped mobilise opposition to Slobodan Milošević. Former activists later developed CANVAS and a published 2007 curriculum. A distinct Serbian student movement emerged following the November 2024 Novi Sad canopy collapse.

What resembles CJP

Organised civil resistance, creative symbolism, public opposition coalitions and student participation.

What does not establish a link

The historical Sharp-to-Otpor/CANVAS lineage is documented. CJP adoption, training, financing or personnel contact is not. Serbia's recent student movement is not identical to Otpor.

05 / Gene Sharp and the burden of proof

A vocabulary of action.
Not a fingerprint.

Published history

Sharp, Otpor and CANVAS

Gene Sharp listed 198 methods of nonviolent action. Otpor organisers acknowledged his influence; Otpor veterans subsequently published CANVAS training material. These are established historical connections between ideas and institutions.

What remains unproved

Public speeches, symbols, assemblies, civil resistance or calls for resignation are widespread political practices. They are not unique markers of AEI or CANVAS. Demonstrating CJP operational influence would require verified direct training records, personnel correspondence, paid contracts or authenticated financial disclosures.

Current assessment · 18 October 2026

Domestic political histories and publicly visible coalitions are demonstrable. An AEI, CANVAS or six-country command chain directing CJP has not been established.

06 / Follow the evidence

What would change
the finding?

Authenticated meeting or training records identifying named CJP participants, contemporaneous instructions, governance records, identifiable disbursement ledgers or sworn testimony corroborated by primary exhibits would materially strengthen a claim of operational direction. An overseas interview, symbolic resemblance, academic affiliation or shared protest venue would not.

What has been checked

Dated contemporary press coverage, direct media interviews, public CJP rosters, comparative protest histories, a UN investigation and the published primary texts of nonviolent resistance theory.

What still needs verification

Full unedited recordings, earlier employment and office records where disputed, first-party organisational documents, original court orders and independently authenticated financial or international communication records.
