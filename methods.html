03 / Methodological checks

Similarity is not coordination.

The 198 methods describe forms of action found across numerous independent political and social movements.

01 / Attribution

Who did it?

An ally's fast, a student's poster or an independent group's flash protest is not automatically a CJP command. Every positive match has actor-scope context.

02 / Time and proof

Did it occur?

Announced marches, a reported intention to disobey a rule and contested allegations of obstruction are not re-labelled as verified completed action.

03 / Influence

What proves a link?

A direct AEI–CJP training, finance or instruction connection would require authenticated contacts and records. A taxonomy match provides no such proof. This site's forecast is a conditional description, not a strategic recommendation.
